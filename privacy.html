Privacy Policy

Maritime Basics
Effective date: October 18, 2026

What this app does

Maritime Basics is a personal app used only by its owner, Aman, to upload his own maritime lesson videos and manage his own playlist on his own YouTube channel (AmanGpt) through the YouTube Data API.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, store, or process any personal data from other people. It is not offered to the public and there are no user accounts.

Google account access

The app requests OAuth access only to the owner's own YouTube channel. The resulting OAuth token is stored privately by the owner and is used only to upload videos and manage playlists on that channel.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with anyone.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access granted to this app can be revoked at any time from your Google Account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
